                                            PREFACE


              The Census data of each Census is published according to the respective
boundaries of districts. sub-divisions and tehsils/talukas at the time of the respective Censu~.
However. when the data over a period of time is to be examined and analyzed. it requires to be
comparable according to the administrative boundaries on a specific date.


               It has been observed that over the period of last censuses there have been certain
changes in the boundaries of administrative units. It was therefore, considered advisable that the
census data of the last censuses may be presented according to the boundary position at the time
of 1998 Census. The census data of all the four previous censuses of Pakistan have, therefore,
been adjusted according to the administrative boundaries as on I st Mar~h, 1998.


              ·The population of some of the districts and tehsils/talukas could not be worked
out due to non-availability of comparable data of Mauzas/Dehs composing these areas and their
population has been sh0wn against the districts out of which the new districts or tehs il s were
created.


               The population of the urban areas has been published as these existed at the time
of the respective Census irrespective of the fact that these were wholly or partly rural at the time
of other censuses.


               The population of urban areas created after the 1981 Census has also not been
shown in the previous censuses, as it could not be worked out due to non-avai labi Iity of the
requisite details of areas forming these urban areas in those censuses.


               The data for the 1951 . 1961 . 1972 and 1998 Census exclude foreigners . The 1981
Census data includes foreigners and their population cannot be excluded because no question on
nationality was asked in the 1981 Census.
