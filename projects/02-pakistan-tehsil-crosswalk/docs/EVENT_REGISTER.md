# Event register: dated district and tehsil changes, 1998 to 2026

Professor Khan asked for the changes in the years they happened, not just at the three censuses. This register lists every district change the crosswalk detected between census frames, every new tehsil, and the districts notified after the 2017 census, each with the year, the source that was read, and a grade: **verified** (an official notification or two independent news reports), **reported** (one news report or a Wikipedia page that cites one), **unverified** (nothing found; the year is blank rather than guessed).

The weights in the crosswalk tables exist only between census frames, because only there did PBS publish populations on both old and new boundaries. The register dates the changes; it does not add weights for in-between years. A user who needs, say, the 2010 district frame can take the 1998 -> 2017 table and keep only the changes dated 2010 or earlier, using the lineage table to see which tehsils each new district took.

## Notes on the census frames
- BALOCHISTAN: The project's 2023 census unit master lists 34 districts for Balochistan (Duki, Surab and Chaman tabulated separately); the districts notified in 2022 (Hub, Usta Muhammad, Karezat) and Lehri (2013, de-notified 2018) were not separately tabulated and sit inside Lasbela, Jaffarabad, Pishin and Sibi respectively. Press counts of 35 to 37 districts in 2021-2022 are inconsistent with each other.
- KHYBER PAKHTUNKHWA: The PBS 2023 census Table 1 for KP lists 35 districts: the 25 districts of 2017 minus Kohistan and Chitral, plus Upper Kohistan, Lower Kohistan, Kolai Palas Kohistan, Upper Chitral, Lower Chitral and the seven former agencies; districts notified but not separately tabulated in 2023: South Waziristan Upper and Lower (Oct 2022), Central Dir (Jan 2023) and Allai (Jan 2023); Paharpur (Nov 2025) and Bar Swat (Jan 2026) post-date the census. No source was found for Hassan Khel, Darazinda or Chamkani ever being made districts; they are a sub-division, a sub-division and a tehsil respectively.
