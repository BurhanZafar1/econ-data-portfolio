Inherited terms. The figures in these tables are published statistics of the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics
and the Population Census Organisation (Censuses 1951 to 2023). The digital source tables were obtained from
Data Darbar (https://darbar.adaad.org/), which redistributes its parsed census tables under CC BY 4.0, and the
2023 polygons are the PBS Digital Census boundaries as redistributed there. The free 2017 polygon layers used
only as checks are from the US Census Bureau (Demobase, public domain) and geoBoundaries (CC BY 4.0). The
scanned PCO publications in raw/census1998_frames/ are official Government of Pakistan publications
redistributed by the Lahore School of Economics repository; they are included for verification of the
transcriptions and remain the property of their publisher. Users should cite the original statistical
agencies as well as this package.
