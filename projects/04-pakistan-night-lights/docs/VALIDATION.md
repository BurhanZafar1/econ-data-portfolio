## The break at the DMSP to VIIRS change
In the harmonized DN files the four provinces' sum of lights falls from 2,260,715 (2013) to 1,273,903 (2014) and 1,254,628 (2016), then returns to 2,403,415 in 2017; the lit share of the land area goes 0.283 (2013), 0.128 (2015), 0.259 (2017). Big cities are little affected, dim rural areas lose most of their light. The pattern is in the source rasters themselves (checked directly on the pixels, with and without the DN > 7 floor), so these three years are flagged in series_note and should not be used for changes across 2013-2017 in series A. Series B shows a milder version at its own sensor change: the four provinces' total falls from 145,543 (2012) to 106,900 (2013) and stays low through 2016 (110,933) before 143,543 in 2017. Pakistan did have severe load-shedding in those years, but because each series dips exactly when its own inputs switch from DMSP to VIIRS, the safer reading is a calibration artifact. Changes measured within one sensor era (1992-2013 and 2017-2024 in A; 1992-2012 and 2017-2025 in B) are the ones to trust; a regression that spans the switch should carry year effects and the sensor_basis column.

## The dim end
In the VIIRS-converted years of series A (2014 onward) many remote districts show a faint glow over a large share of their area that series B does not see at all, for example TORGHAR DISTRICT: A lit share 0.304 (above DN 7: 0.000), B lit share 0.000; AWARAN DISTRICT: A lit share 0.002 (above DN 7: 0.000), B lit share 0.000; SHERANI DISTRICT: A lit share 0.063 (above DN 7: 0.010), B lit share 0.000; UPPER CHITRAL DISTRICT: A lit share 0.482 (above DN 7: 0.019), B lit share 0.000. Dropping pixels at or below DN 7, as the series A authors recommend, removes most of it: across the 135 districts in 2023 the correlation of log sum-of-lights between the two series rises from 0.855 (all pixels) to 0.918 (DN above 7). For the darkest districts use the gt7 columns of series A, or series B.

## Method notes
Exact area-weighted statistics (exactextract): a pixel contributes the share of its area inside the polygon. Pixel areas are computed on the WGS84 sphere from the cell size and row latitude, so "sum of lights in DN.km2 or radiance.km2" is comparable between Punjab and Balochistan. Polygons are the PBS Digital Census 2023 boundaries, with the 2023 census names and codes; 47 tehsil and 8 district polygons had self-intersections that were repaired with a zero buffer (no area change beyond rounding). Units outside the four provinces (Islamabad, AJK, Gilgit-Baltistan) are included and flagged in_four_provinces = False.
