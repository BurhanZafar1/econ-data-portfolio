# Published benchmark

`nchs_db364_figure4.csv` is the data table for Figure 4 of NCHS Data Brief No. 364
(Ostchega Y, Fryar CD, Nwankwo T, Nguyen DT. Hypertension prevalence among adults aged 18
and over: United States, 2017–2018. April 2020), transcribed from
https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/data/databriefs/db364-tables-508.pdf (accessed 2026-11-07).

Age adjusted percent (and standard error) of nonpregnant adults aged 18 and over with
SBP ≥ 130 mmHg, DBP ≥ 80 mmHg, or current antihypertensive medication, standardized to the
projected US 2000 population with age groups 18–39, 40–59 and 60 and over.
